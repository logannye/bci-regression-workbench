About this demo & dataset

Both ridge decoders trained on September 15, 2016 and were evaluated on three later days. The candidate adds three preceding completed neural bins to the baseline’s current bin. All three sessions have lower candidate error; differences are descriptive. This static page runs no models and accepts no uploads.

Derived from Joseph E. O’Doherty, Mariana M. B. Cardoso, Joseph G. Makin, and Philip N. Sabes, Nonhuman Primate Reaching with Multichannel Sensorimotor Cortex Electrophysiology, Zenodo version 2 (2020), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.3854034, licensed CC BY 4.0. This project selected four recording days, binned neural activity, derived velocity, and generated decoder predictions and comparison summaries. The report includes bounded derived sample previews.

An independent project by Logan Nye, intended for Neuralink’s BCI ML engineers. No official affiliation, internal integration, participant evaluation, or interactive-control result is implied. The saved scoring bundle is separate from this hosted preview.
