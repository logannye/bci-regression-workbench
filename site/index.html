Real implanted neural recordings from one BrainGate2 participant performing cursor tasks. Two example ridge models train on the earliest three days and are compared on all twelve later days. The candidate adds three previous neural bins. It has lower inferred-target error on all twelve days; all 324,732 expected samples are scored. Differences are descriptive.

Targets are inferred cursor-to-target displacement, in dataset coordinate units. They are not measured intended velocity. These are new offline example decoders, not the participant’s original online decoder or a reproduction of MINDFUL. Lower proxy error does not establish improved live control. Released threshold crossings include noncausal processing.

Derived from Tsam Kiu Pun, Mona Khoshnevis, Thomas Hosman and colleagues (2024), Measuring instability in chronic human intracortical neural recordings towards stable, long-term brain-computer interfaces, Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.n2z34tn5s, CC0-1.0, release version 6. Transformations: trial masking, training-only feature standardization, current/past-bin designs, two fixed ridge fits, and saved prediction comparison. The report contains bounded derived sample previews.
